# Vision & Purpose

Bornemap is an EV charging station discovery and management platform designed
for Tunisia. It connects EV drivers with charging station operators, enabling
seamless discovery, navigation, and station management.

## Why Bornemap

Tunisia's EV infrastructure is growing. Drivers need a reliable way to find
available charging stations, and station operators need tools to manage their
assets and attract users. Bornemap bridges this gap with a platform that
prioritizes simplicity, reliability, and clear role-based access.

## Platform Roles

- **Public Drivers** — browse stations without authentication
- **Registered Drivers** — authenticated users with personal features
- **Partners** — manage their own stations and chargers
- **Admins** — manage the full platform
